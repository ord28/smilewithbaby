About

Emezie Chinedu, known professionally as Ba byy, is an artist from Anambra State, raised in Onitsha. His journey is rooted in struggle, resilience, and the belief that a dream is worth fighting for. Coming from humble beginnings, Ba byy has experienced the difficult moments that shape a person long before the world sees the result.

"I struggled to be here, in case you wanna know."

That struggle became part of his sound. His music is a reflection of his experiences, emotions, hopes, and the life he has lived. Every song is another chapter of a story still being written. Ba byy isn't here simply to make music. He's here to create something people can feel, remember, and connect with.

This is only the beginning. 🖤
